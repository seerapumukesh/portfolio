Questions

Designed to connect—not create another silo.

Reqentra is being shaped around the real workflows between hiring teams, staffing partners, recruiters, candidates, and contingent workers.

Is Reqentra an ATS or a traditional VMS?

The vision is broader. Reqentra connects the external hiring and workforce ecosystem while supporting workflows that overlap with VMS, ATS, talent CRM, assessment, and workforce management.

Does the platform “own” a hiring manager’s or recruiter’s contacts?

No. The product direction is permission-aware professional networking. Relationships, organization access, candidate consent, and business data boundaries should be explicit rather than treated as one portable company database.

How is candidate fit scoring different from keyword search?

The goal is to evaluate a structured job requirement against skills, experience, client priorities, and assessment evidence, then show the reasoning behind the score so a human can make the decision.

Can one timesheet support multiple vendor layers?

That is a core workflow goal: the worker submits once, while approval, visibility, and downstream routing follow the configured client/vendor/sub-vendor relationship.
