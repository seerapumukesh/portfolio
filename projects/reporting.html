From recurring reports to usable answers

Mukesh Seerapu

A data platform only pays off once people can actually use it without going around it. Before this work, business teams were pulling the same recurring numbers through manual, ad-hoc preparation — every analyst effectively rebuilding the same report logic on their own, with no guarantee two people's numbers would match.

What I built

I connected the enterprise data platform's warehouse to Power BI and Qlik Sense through a reporting layer built on consistent, shared definitions — the same metric meant the same thing whether it showed up in a finance dashboard or an operations one. That consistency was the actual product here, more than any individual report: once the definitions were trustworthy, building a new dashboard stopped being a multi-week exercise in re-deriving logic.

The result

The reporting layer supported 40+ business users directly and cut report generation time by 60%, by removing the manual preparation step that used to sit between "the data exists" and "someone can act on it."
